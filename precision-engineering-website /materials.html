MATERIALS

Material selection matters

Material availability and machinability depend on grade, form, size, certification and project requirements.

Carbon & alloy steels

For strength and general engineering applications. Exact grade to be confirmed from the drawing.

Stainless steel

For corrosion-resistance requirements, subject to grade and machining review.

Aluminium alloys

For lightweight components and machined housings or brackets.

Brass & other alloys

For suitable fittings, bushings and application-specific components.

Do not promise a material grade, certificate, traceability or special process unless you can source and document it.
